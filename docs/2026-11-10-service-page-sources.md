# Service page sources and editorial treatment

Source files inspected in full on 10 November 2026:

- [Custom Manufacturing PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1unBL411u9y733wwELRzAgMh1w2iaIUBq/view)
- [Pilot Plant PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tUY5IgzEYc35PCZ50oPmwqeLa3EFyNED/view)
- [Supplied SEO copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1S-z7OVVM0VTtOG6pYtpei5-6RrXDOKu9/edit)
- [Manufacturing icon variants](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1CHbjRaqWDtn_RopyP6hsYM6q2s90N0ch)

Both PDFs are single long page wireframes, visually inspected in thirds so all sections and text embedded in graphics could be read. Their copy/section order is adapted to the existing Surfactants typography, colours, spacing, responsive grids and shared shell. The five supporting Manufacturing cards were visually transcribed: R&D, regulatory affairs, efficient scale-up, logistics, and ESG/sustainability support. No supporting photography is supplied in these PDFs; the homepage hero images remain exclusive to the hero. Editors can assign optional introduction imagery later.

The blue “I believe the chemistries…” annotation is an editorial instruction, excluded from public copy. Its requested clearer presentation is implemented through a structured technology list. Repeated Manufacturing technology summary text is represented once, alongside all eight supplied technologies. Other source sections are retained: Manufacturing benefits/highlights/overview/four-step process/four assurances/five support cards/scale range/project CTA; Pilot highlights/three benefits/three process steps/seven reactions/ten raw-material entries/four reactors/four operating assurances/three support items/project CTA.

Only obvious extraction artifacts, typography and grammar are normalised: full-scale/scale-up/long-term/work-up hyphenation, joined words, capitalisation, missing apostrophes, “services bridges” → “services bridge”, “Permits & perations” → “Permits & operations”, and “How we could support…” → “How could we support…”. The source 1mt–35mt range is displayed as “1 mt to 35 mt”; 350 C is displayed as “350 °C”. Numeric values and substantive technical terms remain supplied values, including the distinct phosphation/phosphatation spellings and the source's raw-material names. Source certification/licensing claims remain local review copy, pending final content approval.

The supplied SEO titles/descriptions are preserved verbatim. Page content is versioned in src/cms/service-content.json, imported as two fixed Service pages entries. Headings, prose, lists, statistics, reactor details, support imagery, project-form text and SEO are editable; layouts and fixed route identities remain agency controlled. Source import reruns preserve editorial changes and unpublished/trashed entries.

The PDF enquiry areas use the existing validated enquiry form, keeping First/Last Name, Company, Country, Email, optional Job Position/Website, Message, consent, review and receipt steps consistent with Contact. A project message is pre-filled and editable. The final send label uses the supplied “Contact KLK OLEO Surfactants”; receipt means private storage, with external delivery still unconfigured. No separate public enquiry collection or new recipient is added.

Nine selected green SVG variants are validated for active/external content and rasterised with sharp to transparent PNGs fitting within 192 × 192, preserving original colour/aspect ratio. Source URLs and raw/output hashes are recorded in docs/service-page-assets.json. Original PDFs remain source references rather than public downloads; no temporary authenticated download URLs are versioned.
